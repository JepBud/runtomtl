Bike nav for the runner's leg

Keeps the screen on, follows your GPS along the current leg and warns you when you leave the route.

  • Mount the phone and keep it charging. GPS with the screen on uses a lot of battery.
  • The leg changes on its own when the runner hands off in the tracker.
